Game systems & test rules

How the arcade works

Buttons & flows

Game selection, new round, deal, card play, draw, spin, swap, restart, and cosmetic purchase are wired to local prototype logic.

Mathematics

Card ranks, trick winner, simplified spades scoring, legal U-NO moves, slot payline match, and match-3 score are calculated in the browser.

Persistence

Demo balance and cosmetic ownership use browser localStorage. This is not secure cross-device account storage or a server-authoritative wallet.

Release gates

Automated CI, browser/device QA, approved rules, balance abuse tests, accessibility review, and backend validation remain required before release.
