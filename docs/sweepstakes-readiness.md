# Sweepstakes Readiness Package

That’s My Lingo can support a future sweepstakes campaign only after legal, privacy, platform, and backend review. This document is a product and engineering readiness package, not legal advice and not an active sweepstakes launch.

## Activation status

- Current app status: static Vercel entertainment game.
- Sweepstakes status: planning only.
- Cash prizes: not active.
- Purchase-based entries: not active.
- Winner drawing: not active.
- Prize fulfillment: not active.

## Required official-rules fields

- Sponsor legal name and contact.
- Start date, end date, and time zone.
- Eligibility, residency, age, and exclusions.
- No purchase necessary disclosure.
- Alternate Method of Entry (AMOE) with equal odds and substantially equal effort.
- Prize descriptions, winner counts, and approximate retail value (ARV).
- Odds statement.
- Tax responsibility statement.
- Winner notification, affidavit, release, and fallback winner process.
- Privacy policy link and data-use explanation.
- Social platform disclaimers where social actions are used.
- Void where prohibited language.

## AMOE parity rules

If gameplay, community activity, or purchases can generate entries, a free AMOE must provide an equal opportunity to receive entries. AMOE submissions must be timestamped, auditable, rate-limited according to the official rules, and retained with the same rigor as other entries.

## Prize and purchase guardrails

Do not publish prize tiers, wallet credits, purchase-entry bonuses, or sweepstakes marketing copy until reviewed by counsel. Purchase-based entries require special care: the AMOE must be equally available, and odds cannot favor purchasers.

## State and platform review

Before launch, review state registration/bonding thresholds and retail-promotion rules, especially Florida, New York, and Rhode Island. Review California privacy/publicity requirements. Social platforms must be named as not sponsoring, endorsing, or administering the promotion when relevant.

## Static app boundary

The static Vercel game can show planning/readiness information, but it must not run the live sweepstakes. Live entry collection, fraud checks, random drawing, winner verification, prize fulfillment, and record retention require a backend and operations workflow.
