# App Store and Mobile Readiness

That’s My Lingo may expand to iOS and Android, but mobile releases must preserve the same entertainment-only model as the Vercel web build. The product cannot include cash betting, paid spins, deposits, withdrawals, cash-out, cryptocurrency gambling, sports betting, or redeemable prizes.

## App-store listing requirements

- State that the game is entertainment only.
- State that Demo Coins, Lingo Tokens, Loyalty Bucks, Mystery Keys, XP, badges, cosmetics, and rewards have no cash value.
- Avoid words that imply gambling for money, wagering, jackpots paid in cash, real prizes, or withdrawals.
- Use age ratings appropriate for casino-style visuals and simulated reel gameplay.
- Keep screenshots and promo videos aligned with the no-real-money product model.

## Purchase policy

Allowed future purchases:

- Cosmetic avatars
- Profile frames
- Theme packs
- Decorative reel skins
- Supporter recognition
- Merchandise links outside gameplay outcomes
- Digital art/sound packs outside gameplay outcomes

Disallowed purchases:

- Paid spins
- Improved odds
- Cash-equivalent balances
- Prize redemption
- Deposits or withdrawals
- Wagering entries
- Cryptocurrency gambling value

## Mobile technical gates

Before native release, add or verify:

1. Crash reporting disclosures.
2. Analytics disclosures and event data review.
3. Push-notification opt-in copy and quiet-hour rules.
4. Account deletion flow if accounts are enabled.
5. Restore-purchases flow for cosmetic purchases if platform IAP is used.
6. Reduced motion, dynamic text, screen-reader labels, and touch target testing.
7. Privacy policy and terms links in onboarding and settings.

## Review notes

Mobile app reviewers should be able to see that gameplay uses virtual entertainment balances only. Any backend wallet or account system must be server-authoritative, auditable, and separated from purchases that could affect gameplay odds.
